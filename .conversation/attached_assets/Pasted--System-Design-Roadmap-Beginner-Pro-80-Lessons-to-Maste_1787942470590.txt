🚀 System Design Roadmap (Beginner → Pro)
80 Lessons to Master Backend & System Design
This roadmap is designed so that every topic builds naturally on the previous one. By the end, learners should be able to confidently tackle SDE-2/Senior Backend interviews and design production-scale systems.
 
📘 PART I — Foundations (01–08)
Understand what System Design is and the principles behind it.
#	Topic
01	What is System Design?
02	HLD vs LLD
03	Functional vs Non-Functional Requirements
04	Estimating Scale (Users, QPS, Storage, Bandwidth)
05	Scalability (Vertical vs Horizontal)
06	Availability & Reliability
07	Consistency Models
08	CAP Theorem
